02 — RESEARCH

When a modality goes missing

Most multi-modal medical models assume every input arrives. In a real hospital, they don't. AMMFF is built for the case where they don't.

Adaptive Multi-Modal Fusion Framework (AMMFF)

Final-year research · NSBM Green University · PyTorch

AMMFF fuses chest X-ray imaging with 18 structured clinical features to predict the 14 CheXpert pathology labels. Two components carry the idea: Adaptive Fusion Weighting (AFW) re-weights each modality by how much signal it actually carries for a given case, and Missing-Modality Reconstruction (MMR) estimates the absent stream so prediction degrades gracefully instead of collapsing.

Trained across seven architectural variants on MIMIC-CXR-JPG and evaluated against DrFuse and MedFuse, the closest published baselines.
